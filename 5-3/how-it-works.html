FAQs.

Will the video actually look like me?

Watch the film at the top of this page: the presenter is generated end to end, face and voice, and yours is built the same way from ten minutes of you on camera. You see real pages with your clone on them before a single prospect does, and if it is not right, nothing sends.

How will we know if it is working?

Every page is tracked, so you see exactly what comes back: who watched, how far they got, who replied, who booked. Every company, list and offer is different, so we will not promise you someone else’s numbers. What you get is your own, measured from the first batch.

What do you need from us?

A list, or we build one for you. About ten minutes of one person on camera, once; nobody films again after that. And your sign-off at three points before anything goes out.

How fast can it be live?

About two weeks from your recording session to your first pages going out. The session and the brand build come first, then batches move quickly, and every batch after the first is faster still.

Can different parts of the list get different messages?

Yes. Split the list into segments and each segment gets its own script: same recording, different message. Every person still gets their own video and their own page.

Who controls what actually sends?

You do, on every batch. You approve the list, the first page, and the full run: three hard stops before anything goes anywhere.
