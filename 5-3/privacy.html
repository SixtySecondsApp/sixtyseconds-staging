Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026 · Sixty Seconds Ltd

1. Who we are

Sixty Seconds Ltd ("Sixty Seconds", "we", "us") is a company registered in the United Kingdom. We provide AI-powered sales, video and marketing services through this website (sixtyseconds.ai), sixtyseconds.video, use60.com and related landing pages we operate.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at app@sixtyseconds.ai.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us directly - when you fill in a contact, demo-request or lead form: typically your name, email address, company and anything you write in a message field.

Information collected automatically - when you visit our sites: device and browser information, pages viewed, and where your visit came from. If you arrive from an online advertisement, the URL may include a click identifier (such as a Google click ID) and the search term or campaign that brought you here, which we store alongside any form you submit.

We do not knowingly collect data from children, and our services are directed at businesses.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you asked about.
  • To operate, secure and improve our websites.
  • To measure whether our marketing works: if you contacted us after clicking an advert, we record which advert, and if you later become a customer we may report that outcome to the advertising platform so it can measure results. When we do this with Google Ads, your email address is irreversibly hashed (SHA-256) before it is sent, in accordance with Google’s customer data policies.
  • To send you relevant information about our services where you have asked for it or where we have a legitimate interest in contacting your business. You can opt out at any time.

Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are: performance of a contract (or steps you ask us to take before one), legitimate interests (running and promoting our business, measuring our advertising), and consent where required.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf under contract, including: cloud hosting and storage (e.g. Amazon Web Services, Railway), communication tools (e.g. Slack, email providers), our CRM, and advertising platforms for the measurement described above (e.g. Google). Some providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the UK Addendum to Standard Contractual Clauses.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes above: enquiry and lead records for as long as the relationship is active or reasonably prospective, and accounting records for the periods UK law requires. Data we no longer need is deleted.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data erased, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these, email app@sixtyseconds.ai. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Our analytics (Plausible) is cookieless. Advertising and measurement cookies (such as Google and Meta pixels) are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer. Advertising click identifiers (such as a Google click ID) may also be read from the page URL. Our demo form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, an invisible bot-detection service; its handling of data is described in the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum. You can additionally control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always at this address, with the effective date below. Material changes will be flagged on this page.
